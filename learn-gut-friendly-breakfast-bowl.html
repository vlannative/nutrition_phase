Gut-friendly breakfast bowl

A five-ingredient breakfast that's gentle on digestion and keeps you full past 11am — built for anyone in the Gut Health program, but good for everyone.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup lactose-free milk or oat milk
  • 1 small ripe banana, sliced
  • 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Combine oats, milk and a pinch of salt in a small pot. Simmer on low heat for 5–6 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  2. Remove from heat once the oats have thickened to a soft, creamy consistency.
  3. Stir in the ground flaxseed and cinnamon.
  4. Top with sliced banana and serve warm.

Why it works

Oats provide gentle, soluble fibre that's generally well tolerated even during a gut health reset, ripe banana is easier to digest than underripe, and flaxseed adds a small, steady fibre boost without being heavy on the stomach. Lactose-free or plant milk avoids a common trigger for people who are lactose sensitive.
