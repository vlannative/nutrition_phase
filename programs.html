Programs

Structured, multi-week support for specific goals. Two programs to start as more will be added as capacity grows.

Please note: these programs provide nutrition and lifestyle support. They are not medical treatment and do not replace diagnosis or care from a doctor. If you have a diagnosed medical condition, please consult your physician before starting. See the full Nutrition/Health Disclaimer.

Gut Health Reset

Who it's for: People experiencing bloating, irregularity, or ongoing confusion about which foods trigger discomfort.

What's included
Initial assessment, a personalised elimination-and-reintroduction approach, weekly check-ins, and a maintenance plan at the end.
Duration
6 weeks
Format
1 full intake session + 5 weekly follow-ups.
